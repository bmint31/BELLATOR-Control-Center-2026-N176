BELLATOR CONTROL CENTER 1.1.5 — BẢN TIẾNG ANH

Bo cai: BELLATOR-Control-Center-1.1.5-English-Setup.exe
Nguon: BellatorConsole_Setup_V1.1.5.zip ban da cung cap.
May duoc ho tro nhu bo goc: Bellator N176, Bellator N176B, Bellator N176C.

CAI DAT
1. Dong cua so Control Center cu neu dang mo.
2. Chay file Setup bang Run as administrator.
3. Tiep tuc theo huong dan tieng Anh. Bo cai co san .NET Desktop Runtime
   6.0.5 x64 cua bo goc va chi chay no khi thieu .NET 6.
4. Cong cu can mau man hinh tu chay sau khi cai, giong bo goc. Trong luc
   cong cu nay dang hoat dong, giu chuot yen theo thong bao tren man hinh.
5. Mo BELLATOR Control Center tu shortcut.

DIEU KHIEN QUAT
- Current speed: toc do quat hien tai.
- Manual limit: gioi han RPM. Dung nut + / - nhu phan mem goc; moi muc
  duoc gioi han theo che do Quiet, Balanced, Beast hoac Battle.
- Auto: nut Restore Defaults cua ban goc. Nut nay tra hai quat ve dieu
  khien firmware va dat lai tat ca gioi han quat da luu ve mac dinh.
- Manual limit la gioi han toc do, khong phai khoa quat o mot RPM co dinh.
  Khi mo lai app, ban goc van nap lai cac gioi han da luu.
- Khong them fan curve tuy y: chua co bang chung giao thuc va thu nghiem
  phan cung de ho tro chuc nang do.

NHUNG GI DA THAY DOI
- Dich giao dien chinh, hop thoai canh bao, thong bao loi, nhat ky hotkey
  va giao dien cong cu can mau sang tieng Anh.
- Chinh kich thuoc nhan/nut cho chu tieng Anh hien thi day du.
- Lam ro nut Auto va nhan Manual limit theo dung lenh quat da co san.
- Giu cac lenh WMI, gioi han RPM, che do hieu nang, GPU, ban phim va OSD
  cua ung dung goc. Dich vu hotkey va cac thu vien di kem duoc giu lai.
- Bo Setup va Uninstall duoc dong goi lai voi thong bao tieng Anh,
  cung AppId va cung danh sach ba model cua bo goc.
- Mot so ten tep/thu muc noi bo van giu ten goc de phim tat va tai nguyen
  WPF hoat dong. Khong doi cac ten nay bang tay.

KIEM TRA VA GIOI HAN
- Da kiem tra 786 phuong thuc, 21.306 lenh CIL va 116 khai bao P/Invoke:
  luong lenh va loi goi giu nguyen, chi cac chuoi dich duoc thay the.
- Da dung thu giao dien chinh va cac hop thoai bang WPF, kiem tra cong cu
  can mau bang dung .NET Framework; 20 anh OSD goc khong co chu Trung.
- Da giai nen bo Setup va doi chieu du 403 tep thanh phan goc bang SHA-256.
- Chua chay chu ky cai/go va thu dieu khien quat/GPU thuc te cua ban moi.
  Anh xem truoc chi de kiem tra bo cuc, khong phai so lieu may dang chay.
- Day la ban dich dong goi lai, khong co chu ky so cua nha san xuat.
